Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This policy explains how scaleright.ca uses cookies and similar technologies. It should be read with our Privacy Policy.

1. What we mean by cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small text files a site can store in your browser. Similar technologies include localStorage, pixels, and scripts that measure visits. This Site, as it stood on the date above, does not set traditional HTTP cookies of its own. It does use first-party localStorage for essential preferences, and it may load one analytics script (GoatCounter) if you allow it.

2. Your choice

When you first visit, you will see a banner with three clear options of equal weight for the main choice:

  • Accept all cookies — allows essential storage and GoatCounter analytics.
  • Reject non-essential cookies — allows only essential storage; analytics is not loaded.
  • Manage cookie settings — lets you turn analytics on or off, then Save my choices.

You can reopen this at any time with Cookie settings in the footer. Your choice is stored in your browser as sr-cookie-consent. It is not sent to us as a login or identifier.

4. Why analytics is optional

GoatCounter is designed to be privacy-friendly and, by its own documentation, does not set cookies. We still treat it as non-essential and load it only with your acceptance, so you can use the Site without that measurement script. If you reject non-essential cookies, the Site still works, including booking a call and sending email. We simply will not load GoatCounter on later visits unless you change your mind.

Assumption: this description follows GoatCounter’s public default behaviour. We have not independently audited the live scaleright.goatcounter.com account settings.

5. How to change or clear your choices

  • Use Cookie settings in the footer, then save a new choice.
  • Clear this site’s data in your browser to remove theme and sr-cookie-consent. You will be asked again on the next visit.
  • You can also use your browser’s general cookie and site-data controls, or a tracking-prevention setting. Those controls are managed by your browser, not by us.

6. Changes

If we add a new cookie, pixel, or embed, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date, and we will ask for a new choice if the change is material.
