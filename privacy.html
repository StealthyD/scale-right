Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This policy explains how personal information is collected, used, and shared when you visit scaleright.ca (the “Site”) or contact Scale Right. It is written for a Canadian business, with PIPEDA as the primary framework, plus basic information about Quebec’s Law 25, Canada’s Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL), and the GDPR for visitors in the European Economic Area or United Kingdom.

1. Who we are

The Site is operated by Dan Ebanks, doing business as Scale Right, a fractional head of sales practice based in Ontario, Canada. The Site does not publish a registered legal entity name or a street address. If you need that information, email ebanks.daniel@gmail.com.

For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, contact Dan Ebanks at ebanks.daniel@gmail.com. That is the contact email published on the Site and the point of contact for privacy requests.

2. Scope

This policy covers the public website and ordinary ways people reach out through it: email links and a link to book a call on Calendly. It does not replace any confidentiality or services agreement we may later sign with a client. If a written agreement says something different about a specific engagement, that agreement governs that engagement.

This policy describes only the practices we could confirm from the live Site as of the date above. We have not invented other tools or data uses. If something below is an assumption, it is labelled as one.

3. Information we collect

Information you choose to give us

  • Email. The Site has no contact form and no newsletter signup. If you email ebanks.daniel@gmail.com, or use the “Email me” menu (mail app, Gmail, Outlook, or copy address), we receive whatever you send: typically your email address, name if you include it, and the content of your message. Gmail and Outlook compose windows are provided by Google and Microsoft; if you open those links, their own privacy policies apply.
  • Call bookings. “Book a call” is a link to Calendly (calendly.com/ebanks-daniel/scale-right-intro-call). It is not an embedded widget or iframe on this Site. If you book, you leave this Site and provide information to Calendly (for example your name, email address, and any details you type into the booking form). Calendly then shares booking details with us so we can meet with you. See Calendly’s privacy policy.

Information collected automatically

  • Hosting and server logs. The Site is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub and its content delivery network may record standard request data such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested, the date and time, and the referring URL. We do not operate those servers ourselves. How long those logs are kept, and how they are used, is described in GitHub’s privacy statement. This is ordinary hosting, used to serve the Site, keep it secure, and diagnose problems.
  • Analytics (only if you accept). If you choose “Accept all cookies” or allow analytics in “Manage cookie settings,” the Site loads GoatCounter, a privacy-oriented analytics service, from gc.zgo.at, which sends a page-view count to scaleright.goatcounter.com. According to GoatCounter’s privacy documentation (as of June 8, 2025), GoatCounter does not set cookies or store information in your browser. It stores aggregate counts (for example page path, referrer, browser, operating system, language, screen width, and country derived from IP). IP address and the full User-Agent string are not stored in its database; a short-lived in-memory value may be used for up to eight hours to avoid double-counting a visit. GoatCounter’s hosted service stores data on servers in Finland and Germany and is operated from Ireland. Assumption: we have not logged into the GoatCounter dashboard for this policy, so we describe GoatCounter’s default, publicly documented behaviour. If individual pageview logging were turned on in that dashboard, slightly more detailed (still limited) records could be kept; we have not confirmed that setting here.

What this Site does not collect itself

We did not find a contact form, payment form, account registration, marketing pixel, advertising network, or embedded third-party widget on the Site. The photo on the About section (dan.jpg) is hosted with the Site. The Site names the font “Inter” in its style sheet but does not load fonts from Google Fonts or another font service; your device uses a system font.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

This Site does not set traditional HTTP cookies for analytics or advertising. It does use your browser’s localStorage for:

  • your light or dark theme preference (theme), and
  • your cookie and analytics choice (sr-cookie-consent).

Those are first-party, functional items. Analytics (GoatCounter) runs only after you accept it. Details, including a table of what we actually use, are in the Cookie policy. You can reopen Cookie settings from the footer at any time.

Under Quebec’s Law 25, we want to be clear that the only technology on this Site that can contribute to identifying or locating a visitor is (1) ordinary hosting logs, which are needed to operate the Site, and (2) GoatCounter, which is used for audience measurement and is loaded only with your consent as described above.

5. How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • operate, secure, and improve the Site;
  • understand aggregate traffic, if you allow analytics;
  • respond to emails and booked intro calls;
  • prepare for and deliver consulting or fractional sales work if we work together; and
  • meet legal, accounting, or security obligations.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use the Site to run targeted advertising.

6. Consent and legal bases

PIPEDA (Canada). We collect, use, and disclose personal information for the purposes above, with your consent where required, or as otherwise permitted by law. Visiting the Site and sending us a message or booking a call are ways you can provide consent for the related uses. You may withdraw consent, subject to legal or contractual limits, by emailing us.

Quebec Law 25. If you are in Quebec, you have rights of access, correction, and withdrawal of consent, and you may complain to the Commission d’accès à l’information. We do not use personal information on this Site for automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

GDPR / UK GDPR (if they apply to you). Depending on the situation, we may rely on: your consent (analytics, and some follow-up communications); performance of a contract or steps at your request (responding to a booking or inquiry, providing services); legitimate interests (operating and securing a business website, keeping records of conversations we reasonably need); or a legal obligation. You may withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing that already took place.

7. Sharing and service providers

We share information only as needed to run the Site and respond to you:

  • GitHub, Inc. — website hosting (GitHub Pages) and related infrastructure, including its content delivery network.
  • GoatCounter (Martin Tournoij / goatcounter.com) — analytics, only if you accept it.
  • Calendly, LLC — scheduling, if you choose to book a call on Calendly’s site.
  • Email providers. If you email us, your message is received through the inbox for ebanks.daniel@gmail.com (a Google-hosted address). If you open the Gmail or Outlook compose links, Google or Microsoft process that request.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect rights or safety, or in connection with a transfer of the practice (for example if the business is sold), with appropriate safeguards.

8. Transfers outside Canada

The Site is offered from Ontario, Canada, but some providers are outside Canada. GitHub is based in the United States. GoatCounter’s hosted service uses servers in the EU (Finland and Germany) and is operated from Ireland. Calendly is a U.S. company. Google and Microsoft may process email-related data in multiple countries. Those providers are subject to the laws of the countries where they operate, which may not be the same as Canada’s. By using the Site or contacting us, you understand that your information may be transferred and stored in those places.

9. Retention

We keep emails, booking details, and related correspondence only as long as needed for the purpose they were collected — generally, to respond to you, to manage a working relationship, and to meet record-keeping or legal requirements — and then delete or securely archive them. Analytics data is retained according to GoatCounter’s settings and its own policy. Hosting logs are retained according to GitHub’s practices. Theme and cookie choices stay in your browser until you clear them or change them.

10. Your rights

Subject to the law that applies to you, you may ask us to:

  • confirm whether we hold personal information about you, and access it;
  • correct inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • delete information, or restrict or object to certain uses, where the law allows;
  • withdraw consent, including turning off analytics via Cookie settings;
  • receive a copy of information you provided, in a common format (GDPR data portability, where it applies); and
  • lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

In Canada, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada. In Quebec, you can contact the Commission d’accès à l’information. In the EEA or UK, you can contact your local data protection authority.

To make a request, email ebanks.daniel@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling it. Because GoatCounter stores aggregate counts and, by its own description, does not keep a visitor identifier in its database, we may not be able to find or delete a specific person’s analytics record.

11. Commercial electronic messages (CASL)

If you email us or book a call, we may reply about your request. If we later send commercial electronic messages (for example, to offer services), we will do so only with consent where CASL requires it, identify ourselves, and include a way to unsubscribe. We do not operate an email list or newsletter on this Site.

12. Security

The Site is served over HTTPS. We take reasonable steps to protect personal information we hold, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

13. Children

The Site is intended for business audiences, not for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

14. Third-party sites

Links to Calendly, Gmail, Outlook, and other external sites are not under our control. Their privacy practices are their own. You should read their policies before providing information on those sites.

15. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. The current version will be posted on this page.
